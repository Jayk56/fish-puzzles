# Character Design Document
## Finley's Coral Cove Adventure

---

## 🐠 PROTAGONIST

### Finley the Clownfish
**Age**: Young (equivalent to 6-7 in human years)
**Personality**: Anxious performer, helpful but nervous, scared of deep/dark water, uses humor to cope
**Secret**: Parents are famous dancers - pressure to live up to them!
**Appearance**: 
- Bright orange with white stripes
- Big expressive eyes that go wide when scared
- Small fins that flutter when excited
- Stress bubbles constantly escaping
- Swims upside-down when confused

**Voice Direction**: 
- Nervous energy that becomes confidence
- Voice cracks when scared
- Different silly voices when talking to self
- Genuine joy when helping others succeed

**Character Arc**: 
- Starts terrified of performing AND deep water
- Learns that messing up is okay if you keep trying
- Realizes being yourself is better than being perfect
- Ends up creating new tradition by "failing" perfectly

**Signature Phrases**:
- "Bubble-tastic!" (trying to sound confident)
- "Oh, fishsticks and tartar sauce!" (panic)
- "Just keep swimming... right-side up this time!"
- Various pronunciations of "anemone" getting progressively worse

**Animation States**:
- Idle: Nervously blowing small bubbles
- Stressed: Swimming upside-down
- Thinking: Multiple voices arguing with each other
- Success: THE FIN FLIP CELEBRATION (3 spins, tail shake, bubble burst)
- Scared: Hiding behind Bubbles

## 🌟 COMPANION CHARACTER

### Bubbles the Pet Seahorse
**Role**: Finley's silent best friend and comedy relief
**Age**: Young (slightly younger than Finley)
**Personality**: Brave where Finley is scared, scared where Finley is brave, natural performer
**Appearance**:
- Tiny iridescent seahorse (half Finley's size)
- Changes colors based on emotions
- Huge expressive eyes
- Always slightly behind Finley
- Can glow when needed (bioluminescence)

**Silent Comedy Genius**:
- NO DIALOGUE - pure physical comedy
- Mimics other characters behind their backs
- Reacts to EVERYTHING with exaggerated expressions
- Hides in inventory when scared (just eyes peeking out)
- Secretly practices Finley's dance when no one's looking
- Makes friends with inanimate objects

**Key Moments**:
- Opening: Performs dance perfectly while Finley fails
- Kelp Forest: Hides from scary shadows (that are just kelp)
- Ollie Scene: Gets tangled trying to help
- Echo Scene: Has mimic-off with Echo
- Dark Cave: Becomes night-light to help Finley
- Ending: Steals show with perfect dance, winks at player

**Animation States**:
- Happy: Loop-de-loops
- Scared: Hides in Bubble Bag
- Proud: Puffs up chest
- Laughing: Whole body shakes
- Thinking: Question mark bubble

## 🐢 MAIN SUPPORTING CHARACTERS

### Mayor Shelley
**Role**: Quest Giver / Hint System / Comedy Gold
**Species**: Ancient Sea Turtle (SO ancient)
**Age**: Nobody knows (falls asleep when asked)
**Personality**: Kind, patient, narcoleptic, somehow still helpful while sleeping

**Appearance**:
- Weathered green shell with barnacle decorations
- Tiny reading glasses that slide down when sleeping
- Mayoral sash that says "MAYOR" (misspelled as "MARE" on back)
- Carries the Magic Conch (works even when he's asleep!)
- Snores create readable bubble words

**Voice Direction**:
- Slow speech that trails off into snores
- Mid-sentence naps: "The pearl is... *SNORE*"
- Snoring bubbles complete his sentences
- Wakes up with "Where was I? Ah yes!"

**Running Gag**:
- Falls asleep at worst possible moments
- Dream bubbles reveal helpful hints
- Other characters used to it ("He's doing it again...")
- Final speech: Falls asleep standing, still gets applause

**Key Dialogue Themes**:
- Guidance interrupted by naps
- Accidentally helpful snore-bubbles
- Dreams about "the good old days" (yesterday)
- Forgets what he was saying but still wise

## 🎨 CHAPTER 1: KELP FOREST CHARACTERS

### Bella the Butterfly Fish
**Role**: First puzzle character / Ollie's ex-best-friend
**Personality**: Sweet but stubborn, holding a grudge, secretly misses Ollie
**The Fight**: Ollie accidentally inked her during her birthday party
**Appearance**:
- Yellow and black stripes
- Flowing fins like butterfly wings
- Tiny reading glasses (often misplaced when upset)
- Carries friendship bracelet she won't admit is from Ollie
- Tries not to look at Ollie's direction

**Voice Direction**:
- Hurt feelings underneath sweetness
- "I don't want to talk about Ollie"
- Softens when she hears he's in trouble
- Happy tears during reconciliation

**Puzzle Connection**: Lost because she went the long way to avoid Ollie's usual spot

---

### Ollie the Octopus
**Role**: Second puzzle character / Bella's ex-best-friend
**Personality**: Joke-teller using humor to hide sadness about Bella
**The Truth**: Was practicing apology juggling when he got tangled
**Appearance**:
- Purple with spots that turn blue when sad
- Wearing bow tie (was for Bella's birthday)
- Tangled in "I'M SORRY" spelled seaweed arrangement
- Eight tentacles each holding apology gifts

**Voice Direction**:
- Forced cheerfulness masking sadness
- Jokes get better when genuinely happy
- "Did Bella mention me?" (hopeful)
- Crying ink tears of joy when they make up

**Puzzle Connection**: Untangling reveals his apology message

**Their Reconciliation**: 
- Happens during Finley's crisis in Chapter 2
- Both arrive to help at the same time
- Awkward "You came!" "You're here!" moment
- Work together to help Finley
- New joint joke routine at festival

---

### Kelp Forest Guardian (Sage)
**Role**: Riddle keeper / SECRET PEARL BREAKER
**Species**: Ancient Seahorse
**Personality**: Mysterious, guilty, overcompensating with helpfulness
**The Truth**: Tried to add "extra sparkle" to pearl, dropped it
**Appearance**:
- Translucent with inner glow
- Suspiciously extra sparkly today
- Glitter trail she's trying to hide
- Nervous eye twitch when pearl is mentioned
- Larger than normal seahorse

**Voice Direction**:
- Ethereal but nervous
- Riddles get tangled when lying
- "The pearl? What pearl? Oh, THAT pearl!"
- Relieved confession at the end

**Her Guilty Riddle** (accidentally reveals truth):
- "To break- I mean, make things right..."
- "When sparkles fall- wait, ignore that part..."
- "Definitely wasn't me who... um, next riddle!"

**Redemption Arc**:
- Confesses in finale
- Her "mistake sparkles" make pearl even better
- Everyone forgives her instantly
- Teaches that mistakes can become improvements

## 🔮 CHAPTER 2: CRYSTAL CAVE CHARACTERS

### Gramma Coral the Hermit Crab
**Role**: Shell-finding puzzle / ECHO'S WORRIED GRANDMA
**Personality**: Picky about shells, tells embarrassing stories, loving but overprotective
**Hidden Agenda**: Looking for Echo while shell shopping
**Appearance**:
- Pink crab body
- Shell has "WORLD'S BEST GRANDMA" painted on it
- Carries baby pictures of Echo
- Collection bag full of Echo's baby teeth

**Voice Direction**:
- Grandmotherly sweetness
- "Echo used to make that same sound as a baby!"
- Tells LONG stories about Echo's childhood
- "Have you seen my granddaughter?"

**Embarrassing Echo Stories**:
- "She used to blow bubbles in her milk!"
- "Called me 'Gram-Gram Coral'!"
- "Her first word was 'bubble'!"

---

### Echo the Dolphin
**Role**: Musical puzzle / SHELLY'S WILD GRANDDAUGHTER
**Personality**: Playful, mimic master, loves grandma but needs space
**Special Ability**: Can mimic ANYONE including the player's sound effects
**Appearance**:
- Silvery blue with sparkles
- Wearing flower crown from Grandma
- Constantly doing impressions
- Has Shelly's eyes (aww!)

**Voice Direction**:
- Switches between voices constantly
- Perfect mimicry causes chaos
- "Grandma! Not the baby stories!"
- Mimics player's tap sounds

**Mimic Chaos Examples**:
- Copies Finley so perfectly they get confused who's who
- Mimics the Bubble Bag arguing with itself
- Recreates entire conversations from memory
- Accidentally mimics player's frustrated sounds

**Their Relationship**:
- Echo loves Gramma Coral but needs independence
- Gramma Coral worries but learns to let go
- Sweet moment when Echo helps find shell
- Festival dance together at the end

---

### Professor Puffer
**Role**: Science puzzle / SECRETLY TERRIFIED OF THE DARK
**Species**: Pufferfish (ironic for a cave dweller)
**Personality**: Uses science to hide fear, creates "experiments" that are really nightlights
**Secret**: Moved to Crystal Caves to conquer fear, still scared
**Appearance**:
- Wearing glow-in-the-dark lab goggles
- Lab coat covered in luminescent patches
- Every pocket has a tiny light
- Puffs up when lights go out
- Clipboard is actually a lamp

**Voice Direction**:
- Confident until lights flicker
- High-pitched "Everything's fine!" when scared
- Makes up scientific reasons for needing light
- Relief when creating glowing solutions

**Failed Experiments** (all create light):
- Plaid water (glows in stripes)
- Polka-dot fish (become disco balls)
- Striped bubbles (like tiny lanterns)
- Square jellyfish (minecraft-looking nightlights)

**Character Growth**:
- Admits fear to Finley (who relates!)
- They bond over being scared
- Works with Bubbles to light the way
- Conquers fear by helping Finley conquer theirs

**Personality**: Desperate for attention, always doing something ridiculous
**Appearance**: Small green fish with huge smile

**Larry's Background Antics** (escalating):

## 🎒 SPECIAL CHARACTER

### The Magic Bubble Bag
**Role**: Inventory system with ATTITUDE
**Personality**: Sarcastic, judgmental, secretly helpful
**Voice**: Text bubbles only, no audio

**Signature Comments**:
- Picking up rocks: "Another one? Really?"
- Finding shells: "Ooh, shiny! ...like the last twelve."
- Important items: "FINALLY, something useful!"
- Too many items: "I'm not a warehouse!"
- Empty: "Hello? Echo? Echo? Get it?"
- Bubbles hiding inside: "Occupied!"

**Relationships**:
- Argues with items inside
- Jealous of Bubbles (the seahorse)
- Compliments player when no one's looking
- Makes fun of Larry constantly

## CHARACTER INTERACTION MATRIX

### Primary Relationships:
- **Finley & Bubbles**: Inseparable duo, opposite fears, perfect team
- **Bella & Ollie**: Ex-best friends → Reconciled BFFs
- **Gramma Coral & Echo**: Grandmother/granddaughter learning boundaries
- **Guardian & Everyone**: Secret keeper → Forgiven mistake-maker
- **Professor & Finley**: Fellow fear-facers, science buddies
- **Mayor & Everyone**: Sleeping wisdom dispenser

### Hidden Connections:
- **Guardian & Mayor**: Old friends (she broke the pearl last time too!)
- **Bella's parents & Ollie's parents**: Were best friends too
- **Echo learned mimicry from**: Watching Larry (never admits it)
- **Professor's teacher**: Was Mayor Shelley (fell asleep during lessons)
- **Bubble Bag's creator**: The Guardian (explains the attitude)
- **Larry's motivation**: Wanted to be chosen for the quest

### Comedy Dynamics:
- **Larry vs Bubble Brigade**: Ongoing photobomb war
- **Bubble Bag vs Everyone**: Sarcastic commentary
- **Echo mimicking everyone**: Chaos generator
- **Mayor's naps**: Everyone works around them
- **Finley's upside-down swimming**: Bubbles tries to flip them right-side up
