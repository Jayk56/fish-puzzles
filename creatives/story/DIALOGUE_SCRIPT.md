## 📝 Writing Style Notes

### Keep Dialogue:
- Short and punchy (max 2-3 sentences per box)
- Age-appropriate vocabulary
- Positive and encouraging
- Full of wonder and discovery

### Character Voice Consistency:
- Finley: Enthusiastic, curious, helpful
- Mayor Shelley: Wise, patient, grandfatherly
- Bella: Sweet, grateful, slightly worried
- Ollie: Jokey, energetic, grateful
- Sage: Mystical, rhyming, ancient
- Shelly: Polite, particular, artistic
- Echo: Playful, excited, friendly
- Professor: Scientific, excitable, distracted

### Emotional Beats:
- Start with excitement
- Build tension with problem
- Create joy through helping
- Celebrate successes
- End with triumph
