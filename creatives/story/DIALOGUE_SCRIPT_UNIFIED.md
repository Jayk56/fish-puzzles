## 🎭 Character Voice Notes

### Main Characters
- **Finley**: Nervous energy becoming confidence, voice cracks when scared
- **Bubbles**: Silent but expressive, comedy through actions
- **Mayor Shelley**: Slow, trailing into snores, wise through sleep-talk
- **Sage/Guardian**: Ethereal but guilty, riddles that reveal truth
- **Bubble Bag**: Sarcastic text bubbles, secretly caring

### Chapter 1 Characters
- **Bella**: Sweet but hurt, softens through story
- **Ollie**: Forced cheer hiding sadness, genuine joy when reunited

### Chapter 2 Characters  
- **Gramma Coral**: Grandmotherly, loving but overwhelming
- **Echo**: Playful mimic, bonds through copying
- **Professor**: Confident until dark, high-pitched when scared

### Background Characters
- **Larry**: Silent but always present, visual comedy
- **Marina**: Brief but builds pressure
- **Freddy**: Always "fine" regardless of situation
- **Parents**: Proud and understanding, share Finley's quirks

## 📝 Implementation Notes

### Audio Requirements
- All main character dialogue needs full VO
- Bubble Bag is text-only (no VO needed)
- Bubbles is completely silent (expressive animation instead)
- Larry makes no dialogue sounds (visual comedy only)
- Echo needs ability to layer/mix character voices

### Key Sound Effects Needed
- Finley's stress bubbles (varying intensities)
- Magical kelp music (multiple notes for Symphony)
- Tentacle helicopter spinning
- Pearl restoration magic
- Upside-down swimming swoosh
- Bubble Bag text appearance pop
- Echo's mimicry effects
- Guardian's glitter sprinkling
- Mayor's snoring (with bubble pop text)

### Emotional Beats to Emphasize
1. Opening: Nervous anticipation
2. Quest start: Determination through fear
3. Kelp Forest: Growing confidence
4. Lily Pad: Overcoming deep water fear
5. Crystal Caves: Near breaking point
6. Friend rescue: Joy and relief
7. Finale: Celebration and acceptance

This script maintains kid-friendly humor while including all enhanced story elements and character relationships!
