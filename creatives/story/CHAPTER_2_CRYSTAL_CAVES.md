## 🧩 PUZZLE 5: The Perfect Shell

### Puzzle Overview
**Type**: Pattern matching and deduction
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Estimated Time**: 3-4 minutes

### Mechanics
1. **Requirements Phase**:
   - Gramma Coral gives 3 clues about perfect shell
   - Clues displayed in journal

2. **Search Phase**:
   - Explore 3 shell collections
   - 15-20 shells total to examine
   - Each shell has properties:
     - Size (Small/Medium/Large)
     - Color (Pink/Purple/Pearl/Rainbow)
     - Pattern (Spiral/Ridged/Smooth/Spotted)

3. **Testing Phase**:
   - Bring shells to Gramma Coral
   - She gives specific feedback
   - "Too big!" "Wrong color!" "Almost perfect but..."

### Variable Elements (Replayability)

**Variation A**: 
- Needs: Medium, Pink, Spiral
- Location: High shelf collection

**Variation B**:
- Needs: Small, Rainbow, Smooth
- Location: Hidden in sand

**Variation C**:
- Needs: Large, Pearl, Ridged
- Location: Behind crystal formation

### Hint Progression
1. "Remember what Gramma Coral said she wanted."
2. "Check your journal for her requirements."
3. "The perfect shell is in the [specific area]."

## 🧩 PUZZLE 6: Echo Says (Musical Memory)

### Puzzle Overview
**Type**: Audio pattern matching
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Estimated Time**: 3-4 minutes

### Mechanics
1. **Round Structure**:
   - 4 rounds of increasing difficulty
   - Round 1: 3 notes
   - Round 2: 4 notes
   - Round 3: 5 notes
   - Round 4: 6 notes

2. **Input Method**:
   - 5 crystal bells to tap
   - Each makes different tone
   - Visual feedback (crystal glows)
   - Can replay Echo's pattern once per round

### Variable Elements (Replayability)
- Patterns randomly generated from set
- Different starting notes
- Tempo variations (slow/medium)

### Accessibility Options
- Visual mode: Crystals light up in sequence
- Simplified mode: Only 3 rounds needed
- Practice mode: Unlimited replays

### Hint Progression
1. "Watch and listen carefully!"
2. "You can ask Echo to repeat once."
3. "The pattern is [shows first 2 notes]."

## 🧩 PUZZLE 7: Crystal Color Chemistry

### Puzzle Overview
**Type**: Color mixing puzzle
**Difficulty**: Medium-Hard
**Estimated Time**: 3-4 minutes

### Mechanics
1. **Setup**:
   - 5 base crystal powders (Red, Blue, Yellow, White, Black)
   - Mixing bowl in center
   - Target color shown on chart

2. **Mixing Rules**:
   - Primary colors mix normally
   - White lightens colors
   - Black darkens colors
   - Maximum 3 powders per mix
   - Can reset bowl anytime

3. **Feedback**:
   - Professor comments on each attempt
   - "Too dark!" "Close, but too green!" "Almost there!"

### Variable Elements (Replayability)

**Target A**: Aurora Pink
- Solution: Red + White + tiny Blue

**Target B**: Ocean Twilight
- Solution: Blue + Black + tiny White

**Target C**: Golden Sunrise
- Solution: Yellow + Red + White

### Hint Progression
1. "Think about how colors mix."
2. "You might need to lighten/darken it."
3. "Try adding [specific color]."

### Success State
- Perfect crystal forms
- Magical light show
- Professor celebrates

**Success Dialogue**:
- Professor: "Eureka! That's it exactly!"
- Professor: "You have a natural talent for chemistry!"
- Professor: "The Aurora Crystal is perfect!"
- *Crystal resonates and reveals hidden passage*
- Professor: "Fascinating! The crystal is reacting to something!"
- Professor: "There's a passage behind this wall! And... is that light?"

## 🧩 PUZZLE 8: Mirror Light Path

### Puzzle Overview
**Type**: Light beam redirection
**Difficulty**: Medium-Hard
**Estimated Time**: 4-5 minutes

### Mechanics
1. **Elements**:
   - Light source at entrance
   - Rotatable crystal mirrors (5-6)
   - Target pedestal at center
   - Some fixed mirrors for guidance

2. **Goal**:
   - Direct light beam to pearl pedestal
   - Rotate mirrors to correct angles
   - Avoid "dark" mirrors that block light

3. **Progressive Revelation**:
   - Each successful connection lights up path
   - Shows next mirror to adjust
   - Creates rainbow bridge when complete

### Variable Elements (Replayability)
- Mirror starting positions randomized
- 3 different maze layouts
- Optional bonus path to Rainbow Shell

### Hint Progression
1. "Light needs to reach the center."
2. "Rotate the mirrors to redirect the beam."
3. "Start with the mirror closest to the light."
4. Skip: Auto-solves one mirror per use

## Technical Puzzle Notes

### Touch Interactions Required
- Tap to select shells
- Tap to play musical notes
- Drag to pour crystal powders
- Rotate gesture for mirrors
- All puzzles work with single-finger input

### Hint System Integration
- Same 30-second timer for hint appearance
- Visual guides become more obvious
- Skip available after 3 hints
- Professor offers science facts as soft hints

### Accessibility Considerations
- Visual alternatives for audio puzzle
- Color labels for color-blind players
- Simplified mode for younger players
- No precision timing required
